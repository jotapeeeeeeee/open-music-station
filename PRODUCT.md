# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
People who own music files and want a calm, private alternative to subscription catalogs. They are choosing what to listen to at a desk, on headphones, or on a phone.

## Product Purpose
Open Music Station plays user-owned audio locally, remembers listening patterns in the browser, and turns those patterns into understandable recommendations.

## Positioning
The station is explainable and local-first: there is no account, backend, subscription, or opaque feed. The user's files and history stay on their device.

## Operating Context
Users import audio with a picker or drag-and-drop, browse a library, queue tracks, and use a persistent player. The app may include clearly attributed open/legal adapters later.

## Capabilities and Constraints
MVP includes local file import, IndexedDB persistence, metadata editing where possible, search, likes, playlists, queue, player controls, recommendations, not-interested feedback, and local export/import. Demo tracks are synthetic tones with clearly labeled metadata; no proprietary catalog is bundled.

## Product Principles
- Keep ownership visible.
- Explain the next listen.
- Make the first play immediate.
- Prefer resilient local behavior over unreliable network calls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and responsive touch targets are required.
